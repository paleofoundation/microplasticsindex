# Authoritative library review

Date: 2026-10-22. User-confirmed input: microplastics-library, with 17 category folders and regulatory-method. The pre-extraction plan is in full-library-ingestion-contract.md.

All 433 PDF records were inventoried, checked for PDF identity and matched to their supplied SHA-256 values. Every record has a screening disposition in data/library-screening.json. Folder labels, manifest tiers and automatic polymer tags were not accepted as scientific findings. Deeper reading was deliberately selected by direct topic relevance and method interpretability; this is not an exhaustive systematic review of all papers.

The site has 17 category pages, 49 source records and 80 traced values. The latter include occurrence/release results, size windows, sample counts and explicitly labeled method-scope values. Every value retains the original artifact hash, units, statistic/denominator, conditions, material, size context and PDF locator. Source pages expose those locations. Primary PDFs, full text extracts and source-table images are not redistributed.

## Coverage and interpretation

Food storage adds direct PP-container extraction and specified container-contact experiments from the authoritative food-packaging folder. The already reviewed PS-container artifact matches the same folder. Earlier supplementary food-wrap, cooking/ice-bag and heat-sealing sources retain separate archive provenance. Sequential flushing, single-contact tests, film-area counts and bag-material characterization remain distinct. No ordinary zip-top bag release rate or repeated-household-tub-use rate is invented.

Baby-wipe measurements retain total fibers and retained material rather than calling cellulose or lotion plastic. Infant-bottle water experiments remain distinct from concentrated ethanol abrasion extracts. Tea-bag cut-square aging and detection frequency remain distinct from ordinary brewing and particles per cup. Bottled-water and salt counts retain source-specific size and classification limits, including regenerated cellulose where the authors included it.

Beer estimates use a pooled chemical subsample across food categories and substantial extrapolation. Honey counts retain limited chemical confirmation. Tap-water results distinguish blank-limited objects from chemically confirmed plastics. Cutting boards have one direct primary source. Cosmetic/personal-care values use specified table entries with the source's table/prose conflicts disclosed; unsuccessful cosmetic-product extraction attempts are excluded.

Infant puree has only a review in the supplied folder. Its page provides context and states the direct evidence gap, without a numerical puree result. Fish/seafood pages use edible-tissue scope. Wildlife is a narrow gull-feces indicator study with missing quantitative blanks and partial chemical confirmation. Air, settled dust and human tissues have separate exposure-topic pages; detection is not source attribution or proof of disease. Textile fiber mass, counted fibers, fibrils and nanoparticle estimates retain separate measurement bases.

## Regulatory-method review

All nine documents are represented as legal instruments, official methods, policy material, technical reports, a scientific statement or a research framework according to their actual authority. The California handbook supplied copy is explicitly dated before adoption; proposed implementation dates are not asserted as current. California's current official index was checked and separately identifies the adoption resolution and revised methods. The EU water decision supplies a methodology, not a particle safety ceiling. The synthetic-polymer-microparticle restriction is not described as a blanket ban on article shedding. The EFSA statement is dated 2016, with no inference that its historical legislative description is current worldwide law. The Coffin framework is not treated as an occurrence study or clinical threshold.
